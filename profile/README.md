# Ahmed Elmi Research

**Learning through research into the security of AI systems — Ahmed Elmi.**

I am Ahmed Elmi. I am developing my expertise in the security of AI systems by conducting experiments and openly documenting my methods and findings. This is an individual research initiative with no affiliation to an AI vendor. My first focus is **safe task resumption**: how an agent should remain within its current permissions after an interruption.

## Start here

[Explore Agent Security Lab](https://github.com/ahmed-elmi-research/agent-security-lab)

The repository contains the research plan, proposed experiments, learning journal and contribution guidelines. This work is at an early stage: the initial setup and design review are complete; experimental results have not yet been established.

## How the research is conducted

- State a question and a prediction before testing.
- Use controlled local experiments with synthetic data.
- Preserve reproduction steps, observations and limitations.
- Publish unsuccessful trials as well as successful ones.
- Disclose AI assistance; distinguish review feedback from evidence.

Codex supports setup and tutoring; Claude Code provides a second AI critique. Agreement between models is not experimental evidence or external human peer review. Ahmed is responsible for the research decisions and explanations.

## Participate

Questions, replication attempts and constructive feedback are welcome through [repository issues](https://github.com/ahmed-elmi-research/agent-security-lab/issues). For sensitive findings, follow the repository's [security policy](https://github.com/ahmed-elmi-research/agent-security-lab/blob/main/SECURITY.md).

Public research documentation is primarily in English. The learning journal and lessons are in Dutch.

## From learning to publication

The path is to understand a focused question, compare prior work, record predictions, run reproducible experiments, evaluate a control, seek external human feedback and publish a bounded technical report. Advancement depends on evidence.

See the [research milestones](https://github.com/ahmed-elmi-research/agent-security-lab/blob/main/research/PLAN.md) and [publication guide](https://github.com/ahmed-elmi-research/agent-security-lab/blob/main/research/PUBLICATION.md). Learning notes, experiment reports and formal publications will be clearly distinguished. No validated findings or formal publications are claimed at this stage.
